Influencer marketing has always been about more than likes and followers, it’s about driving real business outcomes. But until recently, most brand partnerships were built around flat fees and one-off campaigns, making ROI tough to measure. Now, in 2025, the industry is experiencing a major shift: affiliate-first influencer marketing.
