# GRIN

> GRIN is a creator marketing platform for brands. Gia is the AI agent inside GRIN that helps operate creator discovery, outreach, programs, payments, content tracking, and reporting while teams approve consequential actions.

## Direct Answers

- **What is GRIN?** GRIN is a creator marketing platform that brings creator discovery, relationship management, campaigns, content, payments, and measurement into one connected system.
- **What is Gia?** Gia is GRIN's AI agent for creator marketing. She performs creator-program work and brings key decisions involving messages, money, contracts, or brand risk to people for approval.
- **Who is GRIN for?** GRIN serves brands running creator, influencer, affiliate, gifting, paid, and UGC programs. GRIN also provides a free Creator App where creators manage opportunities, deliverables, commissions, and payments.
- **How is GRIN priced?** GRIN offers monthly usage-based plans from Free through Complete. Credits meter the work Gia performs, not seats or creator-list size. Growth, Scale, and Complete also include GRIN Classic capacity.
- **What scale has GRIN supported?** GRIN's cleared company-scale proof is $1B+ in creator-driven partnerships, a network of 700K+ creators, and $215M+ earned by creators.
